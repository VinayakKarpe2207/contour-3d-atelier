The Master Graphite Codex

In classical Renaissance draughtsmanship—from Leonardo da Vinci’s anatomical studies to Albrecht Dürer’s geometric engravings—a drawing is not simply a flat silhouette. It is an exploration of volume, mass, and tension realized through rhythmic cross-hatching and architectural guide axes.

CONTOUR transforms this tradition into an interactive spatial studio across 10 Master Studies. Viewed directly along the orthogonal axis (click "Align 2D Perspective"), the strokes collapse into a graphic draughtsman's sketch. Upon orbit, the layered graphite curves expand into third-dimensional space, revealing internal mechanisms, organic venation, and structural anatomy.

“Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication, and the line is its purest vehicle.” — Leonardo da Vinci

Interaction Guide:

  • Drag / Swipe: Orbit the 3D graphite sketch freely in space.
  • Align 2D Perspective: Snaps the camera into the exact angle where 3D depth collapses into a 2D drawing.
  • Live Re-Sketch: Watch the piece materialize live stroke-by-stroke with authentic tactile graphite audio.
  • Cross-Hatch Shading: Toggle volumetric shadow hatching on/off to analyze the underlying structural contours.
  • Freehand Sketch Studio: Draw your own 3D pencil strokes freely in spatial coordinates.
