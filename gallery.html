Three Codices: Graphite, Sumi-e & Sacred Architecture

CONTOUR unites three transcendent spatial traditions across 30 Master Studies:

Volume I • The Renaissance Graphite Codex: Studies in linear perspective, architectural cross-hatching, and kinetic mechanical anatomy inspired by Leonardo da Vinci and Albrecht Dürer.

Volume II • The Sumi-e & Zen Ink Codex: Ancient Japanese ink wash (*Suibokuga*) where a single mindful brush stroke captures the vital essence (*Ki / Qi*) of nature through calligraphic pressure, flying-white texture (*Hihaku*), and negative space (*Ma*).

Volume III • Sacred Temple & Pagoda Architecture: Classical timber joinery (*kigumi*), multi-tiered sweeping eaves (*sori*), sacred 9-ring spires (*sorin*), and sacred pavilions spanning Kiyomizu-dera, Kinkaku-ji, Itsukushima, and Tōdai-ji.

“The wooden joint that holds without a single nail yields to the earthquake and stands for a thousand years.” — Japanese Master Temple Carpenter Tradition
