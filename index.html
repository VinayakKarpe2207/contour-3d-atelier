CURATORIAL MANIFESTO

The Line as Spatial Architecture

In traditional draughtsmanship, a pencil stroke is confined to a flat plane of cotton vellum. Yet from Leonardo da Vinci’s anatomical studies to Albrecht Dürer’s geometric engravings, great draughtsmen always perceived the line as an armature of mass, tension, and volume.

CONTOUR liberates drawing from the page. Built with WebGL shaders and procedural spatial curves, every contour, cross-hatch shading stroke, and construction guideline is rendered in real third-dimensional depth.

“A line is a dot that went for a walk into the third dimension.” — Adaptation of Paul Klee, Pedagogical Sketchbook
